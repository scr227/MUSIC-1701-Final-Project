Instruments are a frequently mentioned theme within Springsteen's music, with his lyrics often referring to guitars or other instruments as a means of bringing people together. His frequent exploration of what it means to play an instrument within his lyrics exists alongside uniquely meticulous attention to instrumentation within his music as a whole. The Hammond organ's presence in "Backstreets" follows a dense and somewhat tumultuous history in the decades between the instrument's invention and the song's release. Despite a series of lawsuits surrounding its authenticity and criticism for its supposedly "unnatural" sound, the Hammond organ became a staple of jazz, blues, and rock music as early as the 1940s (Kelly 2019). The Hammond organ, which serves a highly distinctive purpose within the song's narrative, helps embody many of the qualities that Springsteen sought to project throgh the song. As a whole, each element of "Backstreets" is the culmination of decades of cultural, societal, and technological development.
